FestOS synthetic data pack

This pack is intentionally imperfect and should be treated as operational data, not a clean database. Teams should state assumptions, preserve uncertainty, make trade-offs visible, and allow manual overrides with reasons.

The files contain incomplete fields, inconsistent labels, timing anomalies, duplicate-looking records, capacity pressure and cross-file conflicts. Not every inconsistency has one predetermined resolution. Do not treat blanks as consent, gender, dietary clearance or eligibility.

Dates are synthetic and centred on the Aarohan fest window around 30 October–1 November 2026. Contact numbers are fabricated.
